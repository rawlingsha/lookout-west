As governments and businesses tried to cushion the disruption, they drew on emergency reserves and commercial inventories. By the end of August, observed global oil stocks were 507 million barrels lower than at the start of the war. That's a historic drawdown: about one-sixteenth of the observed global oil inventories consumed in six months.</p>

<section className="energy-passage energy-passage--diesel" aria-labelledby="diesel-heading">
  <h2 id="diesel-heading">Diesel has another problem.</h2>

Middle Eastern fuel exports have been disrupted, while damage to Russian refineries has reduced another source of diesel. Other refineries can produce more, but there is only so much extra capacity available.
Diesel prices also rose much faster than crude oil in early September, a sign that the squeeze was hitting refined fuel as well as the oil going into it. [IEA refining assessment](https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-september-2026)

On September 14, on-road diesel averaged $7.25 a gallon on the West Coast and $6.07 in the Rockies. Farmers may pay different prices because agricultural diesel can face different taxes and contracts. [EIA diesel prices](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/)

For farms, trucking firms and builders, a fuel spike can eat into the profit on jobs priced before diesel went up. Some businesses pass on the increase; others absorb it or cut activity. Higher diesel prices do not translate dollar-for-dollar into higher grocery prices because transportation is only one part of the cost.

</section>

<section className="energy-passage energy-passage--revenue" aria-labelledby="oil-money-heading">
  <h2 id="oil-money-heading">Where the extra oil money goes</h2>

New Mexico’s August forecast directs substantial excess oil revenue into trust and permanent funds. That can support services over years, while limiting how much of today’s windfall immediately enters the operating budget. A larger state balance sheet is not a direct payment to residents. [New Mexico forecast](https://www.nmlegis.gov/Handouts/ALFC%20082526%20Item%206%20Preliminary%20FY26%20General%20Fund%20Revenues%20FY27-FY30%20Revenue%20Outlook%20and%20General%20Fund%20Financial%20Summary.pdf)

Alaska’s spring forecast raised its fiscal 2027 unrestricted revenue estimate $510 million from the previous December because of higher expected oil prices and slightly higher production. It was a forecast revision, not cash collected or an estimate of the September disruption. [Alaska Department of Revenue](https://dor.alaska.gov/docs/default-source/press-releases/2026-003-dor-press-release-spring-2026-revenue-forecast.pdf?sfvrsn=ff0ad4e9_1)

Wyoming, Colorado, Utah, Montana and California also produce oil. [EIA state production data](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/pet_crd_crpdn_adc_mbblpd_m.htm) Private gains depend on ownership and contracts. Current-price sellers and royalty owners can benefit; some earnings leave the state, and previously locked sale prices mute the immediate upside.

Producing oil nearby does not guarantee cheap gasoline: sellers face wider market prices, and crude still needs refining and delivery. Local production can reduce some transport costs but cannot remove global competition. [EIA oil pricing](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/oil-and-petroleum-products/prices-and-outlook.php) · [EIA regional fuel costs](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/gasoline/regional-price-differences.php)

<section className="energy-passage energy-passage--outlook" aria-labelledby="price-risks-heading">
  <h2 id="price-risks-heading">What could still keep prices high</h2>

EIA’s September outlook used assumptions finalized September 3. Its projection of improving oil flows preceded the subsequent attacks and September 22 partial restart of Saudi Arabia’s East–West pipeline. The current-state visual above includes that later reporting; the earlier forecast is a dated conditional outlook. [EIA forecast and cutoff](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/) · [Reuters, September 22](https://www.gulf-times.com/article/733893/region/saudi-arabia-restarts-east-west-oil-pipeline-sources-say)

Autumn usually eases gasoline demand and permits cheaper seasonal formulations. That helps if routes stabilize; another refinery or shipping outage could outweigh it. [EIA gasoline seasonality](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/gasoline/price-fluctuations.php)

Diesel faces harvest demand, refinery maintenance, low distillate stocks and unusually wide refining spreads through November. Lower crude alone would not repair a product shortage. [EIA petroleum-products outlook](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/report/petro_prod.php)

Several weeks of better supply would strengthen the case for relief; another outage would weaken it. Cheaper oil caused by a broader slowdown could arrive with weaker business income.
